BUILDING A BETTER PUNE THROUGH REGULAR CLEANUP DRIVES


Our Mission

  • Protect the environment through regular cleanup campaigns
  • Communicate to the public about civic responsibility for the environment

Our Motto 

 Be part of something that gives back, with every step, with every wrapper, every Sunday

Our Initiaves

Cleanup drives across Pune city

Regular cleanup drives across Pune in collaboration with local organisations and volunteers every Sunday.

Civic Awareness Campaigns

Spreading the word on social media and encouraging citizens to not litter, posting about environmental responsibility.

Community Volunteer Building

Mobilising and training new volunteers by building a WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn and Reddit community, onboarding people who want to contribute regularly.

Tree plantation drives across Pune city

Planting native trees across Pune to restore green cover and support local biodiversity.
