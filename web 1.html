Who are we ?

BPTSeva (Baner Pashan Tekdi Seva) is a community-driven initiative in Pune dedicated to preserving, cleaning, and protecting Pune city from litter and garbage. This initiative began from the Baner Pashan tekdi. Through sustained cleanliness drives and active citizen participation, BPTSeva has mobilised 200+ volunteers, collected over 5000kg of waste, and cleaned 8–10 sq. km of natural terrain. Founded with the belief that small steps create big change, BPTSeva works to inspire citizens of Pune and beyond to take ownership of their local environment. Join us at BPTSeva in making a difference !
